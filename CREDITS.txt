Moribund - web demo (Ashwick and the Weeping Graves)
Code, narrative and design: the Moribund project. Built with Godot Engine 4.7 (MIT, godotengine.org).
This demo contains no Diablo / Diablo II material: every image and sound below is open-licensed.
